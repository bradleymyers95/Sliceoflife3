Why it works

Built for participation, not performance

Hosts share what's already true

No script to follow. Every listing is a real routine — a recipe, a craft, a task, a weekly habit — that a local person was already doing, and is willing to open up.

Small groups, low pressure

Most experiences hold 2–6 people. You show up, you help, you ask questions. There's no performance to sit through — you're part of what's happening.

Money stays close to home

Hosts set their own contribution — often just enough to cover ingredients or materials. This isn't a tour company; it's neighbors, and the arrangement stays that simple.
