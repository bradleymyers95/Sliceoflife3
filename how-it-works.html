The short version

Three steps. No middlemen, no performance.

Whether you're looking to join something or open your own routine to visitors, the process is the same on both sides: it starts with an honest listing and ends with people actually meeting.

For guests

Joining an experience

01 / Browse

Find something real

Filter by place or kind of routine — cooking, craft, language, community work. Every listing is written by the person hosting it, in their own words.

02 / Message

Say hello first

Send a short note before you book. Hosts want to know who's coming — this isn't a checkout page, it's an introduction.

03 / Show up

Join in

Arrive on time, bring what's asked (usually nothing), and take part. Most hosts ask for a small contribution to cover materials — paid directly, no platform cut.

For hosts

Opening up your routine

01 / List it

Describe what you already do

Ten minutes, plain language: what happens, how long it takes, how many people fit comfortably. No professional photos required — clarity beats polish.

02 / Set your terms

You're in control

Pick your own schedule, group size, and whether you ask for a contribution. You can pause or close your listing whenever you like.

03 / Host

Welcome people in

Guests message you before booking, so nobody shows up as a stranger. You decide who fits your routine — this stays a two-way invitation, not a ticket sale.

Ground rules

What keeps this honest

Verification

Real identity, both sides

Guests and hosts confirm ID before their first meet-up. Not bureaucracy for its own sake — just enough so everyone knows who they're meeting.

No staging

Listings are routines, not shows

We don't allow experiences built purely for visitors — every listing has to be something the host would be doing anyway.

Fair terms

Contributions go to the host

Slice Of Life takes a small flat fee to keep the platform running. Whatever a guest pays beyond that goes straight to the person hosting.

Or, if you'd rather host, get in touch and we'll help you set up your first listing.
