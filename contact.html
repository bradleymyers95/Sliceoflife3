Before you write in

A couple of quick answers

Guests

Can I request a city that isn't listed yet?

Yes — tell us where you're headed and we'll flag it to hosts in that area, or let you know if something's already in the works.

Hosts

Is there a cost to list an experience?

No upfront cost. We take a small flat fee only when a guest books, so there's no risk in listing something and seeing what happens.

Everyone

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within two business days. Hosting inquiries may take a little longer while we do identity verification.
