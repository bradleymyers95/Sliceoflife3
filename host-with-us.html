Getting started

From routine to listing in three steps

01 / List it

Describe what you already do

Ten minutes, plain language: what happens, how long it takes, how many people fit comfortably. No professional photos required — clarity beats polish.

02 / Set your terms

You're in control

Pick your own schedule, group size, and whether you ask for a contribution. Pause or close your listing whenever you like.

03 / Host

Welcome people in

Guests message you before booking, so nobody shows up as a stranger. You decide who fits your routine.

FAQ

Questions hosts usually ask

Money

Do I have to charge anything?

No. Some hosts ask for a contribution to cover ingredients or materials, others host for free. It's entirely your call.

Safety

Who are the guests?

Every guest verifies their identity before messaging hosts, and you'll always exchange a message before anyone books.

Commitment

How often do I need to host?

As often as you like. Once a week, once a month, or a one-off — your calendar, your call.

Ready to open the door?

Create your host profile and publish your first listing — it takes about ten minutes.
